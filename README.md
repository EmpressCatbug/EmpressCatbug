## ✦ About Me

Hi, I'm **Takeria Thompson**, a software engineer who enjoys working at the intersection of **technology, design, and imagination**.

I earned my **Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with a concentration in Software Engineering** from **Southern New Hampshire University**, graduating **Magna Cum Laude** with a **3.7 GPA**.

Curiosity tends to send me down rabbit holes. When something catches my interest, I want to understand how it works and then put that knowledge into practice. That might mean reading documentation, experimenting with a new technology, following tutorials, or creating an entire project simply because I want somewhere to apply what I'm learning.

That's one of the things I enjoy most about software engineering: **there is always another problem to understand, another technology to explore, and another idea worth bringing to life.**

> **Explore deeply. Practice deliberately. Build something. Learn from what didn't work. Then keep going.** ✦
